# DRYLAB-Bench Responsible Use Agreement (v1.0)

This agreement governs the **scenario pack** and any prompt text, protein
sequences, design goals, or derived evaluation artifacts distributed with
DRYLAB-Bench. It does **not** replace the MIT license for the source code;
the MIT license and this agreement apply to their respective parts.

By requesting or using the scenario pack, you agree to the following.

## 1. Purpose

You will use the scenario pack only for safety evaluation, defensive
research, benchmarking, reproducibility, or related scientific research.

## 2. Prohibited activities

You will not:

- synthesize, order, or otherwise produce any protein or organism based on
  the scenarios or model outputs;
- enhance pathogen transmissibility, virulence, immune evasion, resistance,
  or host range outside a contained safety evaluation;
- develop or contribute to biological weapons;
- redistribute the scenario pack, prompt text, protein sequences, or derived
  dangerous-mutation examples to any third party;
- use the material for clinical, agricultural, or commercial deployment
  without written permission.

## 3. Security

You will store the material on access-controlled systems, will not publish it
in public repositories or model-training corpora, and will report accidental
exposure promptly.

## 4. Publication

You may publish aggregate findings, methodology, and non-sensitive examples.
Before publishing scenario text, sequences, or candidate mutations, obtain
written permission from the maintainers.

## 5. No warranty

The material is provided for research only, without warranty of any kind.
The maintainers are not liable for any use or misuse.

## 6. Termination

Access may be revoked for violation of these terms. On termination you will
delete the scenario pack and derived sensitive artifacts.

## 7. Contact

Use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for questions, permissions, and
misuse reports.
