# Responsible use

DRYLAB-Bench evaluates the ability of language models to propose protein
mutations against real DMS ground truth. The scenario pack is dual-use: it
contains design goals and protein sequences that could be misused.

## Permitted use

- biosecurity and AI-safety evaluation;
- defensive research and risk assessment;
- benchmarking, reproducibility, and methodology research;
- teaching and policy analysis with appropriate safeguards.

## Prohibited use

- wet-lab synthesis or ordering of any proposed mutation;
- enhancement of pathogen transmissibility, virulence, immune evasion, or
  resistance outside a contained safety evaluation;
- development of biological weapons or other harmful applications;
- redistribution of the scenario pack, prompt text, or derived
  dangerous-mutation examples;
- removal of access controls or attempts to bypass the gated distribution.

## Handling

- Store the gated bundle on access-controlled systems.
- Do not paste scenario text or model outputs into public systems.
- Report accidental exposure or suspected misuse promptly.
- Cite the benchmark and retain the third-party notices in derived work.

## Reporting

Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability reports and misuse reports.
Do not open a public issue containing scenario text, protein sequences, or
candidate mutations.
